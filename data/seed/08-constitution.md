id: constitution
title: Chairman of the Drafting Committee
type: Curator note
date: 1947–1950
place: New Delhi
---
On 29 August 1947 the Constituent Assembly appointed a Drafting Committee to prepare the draft Constitution of India, with Ambedkar as its chairman. He also became independent India's first Law Minister.

He piloted the draft through the Constituent Assembly debates, explaining and defending its provisions clause by clause. The Constitution was adopted on 26 November 1949 and came into force on 26 January 1950.

Provisions associated with his work include the abolition of untouchability, fundamental rights, and reserved representation for Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes. He is widely referred to as the chief architect of the Indian Constitution.
