---
id: mahad-satyagraha
title: The Mahad Satyagraha
type: Curator note
date: 1927
place: Mahad, Konkan
---
On 20 March 1927 Ambedkar led a large gathering at Mahad to assert the right of untouchables to draw water from the Chavdar Tank, a public water tank that had been legally opened to all but was denied to them in practice. The event is remembered as the Mahad Satyagraha.

Later that year, on 25 December 1927, a copy of the Manusmriti was publicly burned at Mahad as a protest against the text's sanction of caste hierarchy.

Mahad is widely regarded as the first major organised movement for civil rights of the depressed classes, and it became a model for later campaigns such as the Kalaram temple entry movement at Nashik from 1930.
