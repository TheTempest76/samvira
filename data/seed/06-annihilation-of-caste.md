title: Annihilation of Caste
type: Curator note
date: 1936
place: Lahore; Bombay
---
In 1936 Ambedkar prepared a speech for the annual conference of the Jat-Pat Todak Mandal, a Lahore-based reform group. The organisers withdrew the invitation after reading the text, and Ambedkar published it himself as "Annihilation of Caste".

The essay argues that caste cannot be reformed from within and must be dismantled, and it criticises the religious sanction given to caste. It is among his most widely read works and has been reprinted and translated many times.

The same year he founded the Independent Labour Party, which contested the 1937 provincial elections in the Bombay Presidency.
