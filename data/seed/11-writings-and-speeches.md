type: Collection note
date: 1979–present

The Government of Maharashtra began publishing "Dr. Babasaheb Ambedkar: Writings and Speeches" in 1979, gathering his books, essays, speeches, evidence before commissions and unpublished manuscripts. The series was later reissued by the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment, and scans are available through the Internet Archive.

This collection is the intended primary source for the archive. Drop the volume PDFs into data/docs and run the ingest script to make them searchable on the kiosk.
