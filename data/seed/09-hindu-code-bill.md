id: hindu-code-bill
title: The Hindu Code Bill and resignation
type: Curator note
date: 1951
place: New Delhi
---
As Law Minister, Ambedkar prepared the Hindu Code Bill, which sought to codify and reform Hindu personal law, including rights of women in marriage, divorce and inheritance.

The bill met strong opposition in Parliament and was stalled. In 1951 Ambedkar resigned from the cabinet, citing among his reasons the failure to pass the bill. Its main provisions were later enacted in the mid-1950s as separate acts.

He later served as a member of the Rajya Sabha from 1952.
