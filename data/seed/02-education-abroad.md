title: Higher education at Columbia and the London School of Economics
type: Curator note
date: 1913–1927
place: New York; London
---
With a scholarship from the Gaekwad of Baroda, Ambedkar went to Columbia University in New York in 1913. He received an MA in 1915. His doctoral work on provincial finance under British rule was published as "The Evolution of Provincial Finance in British India" in 1925, and Columbia awarded him the PhD in 1927.

He then studied at the London School of Economics, receiving an MSc and, in 1923, a DSc for the thesis later published as "The Problem of the Rupee". He also trained in law at Gray's Inn and was called to the bar.

This training abroad in economics, law and political science shaped his later work on labour policy, public finance and constitutional design.
