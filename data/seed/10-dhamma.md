---
id: dhamma
title: Conversion to Buddhism
type: Curator note
date: 1956
place: Nagpur
---
On 14 October 1956, at Nagpur, Ambedkar formally embraced Buddhism together with a very large number of his followers. The site is now known as Deekshabhoomi.

He died on 6 December 1956 in Delhi, weeks after the conversion. His final work, "The Buddha and His Dhamma", was published in 1957.

He was posthumously awarded the Bharat Ratna, India's highest civilian honour, in 1990.
