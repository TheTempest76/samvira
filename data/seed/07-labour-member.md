id: labour-member
title: Labour Member and the Scheduled Castes Federation
type: Curator note
date: 1942–1946
place: Delhi
---
In 1942 Ambedkar founded the All India Scheduled Castes Federation. The same year he joined the Viceroy's Executive Council as Labour Member, a post he held until 1946.

As Labour Member he worked on labour welfare and industrial relations, and is often credited with measures such as reducing the factory working day to eight hours.

In 1945 he founded the People's Education Society, which opened Siddharth College in Bombay in 1946. Major writings of this period include "Who Were the Shudras?" (1946), followed by "The Untouchables" (1948).
