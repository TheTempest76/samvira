title: Mooknayak and the Bahishkrit Hitakarini Sabha
type: Curator note
date: 1920–1924
place: Bombay
---
In 1920 Ambedkar started the Marathi fortnightly "Mooknayak" (Leader of the Voiceless), one of several newspapers he founded to give depressed classes a public voice. Later papers included "Bahishkrit Bharat" and "Janata".

In 1924 he founded the Bahishkrit Hitakarini Sabha, an organisation to promote education, social welfare and political awareness among the depressed classes. Its guiding message is often summarised as "educate, agitate, organise".
