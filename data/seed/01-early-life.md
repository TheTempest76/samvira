title: Early life and schooling
type: Curator note
date: 1891–1912
place: Mhow; Satara; Bombay

Bhimrao Ramji Ambedkar was born on 14 April 1891 at Mhow, a military cantonment in present-day Madhya Pradesh, where his father Ramji Sakpal served in the British Indian Army. The family belonged to the Mahar community, which was treated as untouchable under the caste order of the time.

As a schoolboy he experienced exclusion directly: children from untouchable families were often made to sit apart and could not freely use common water sources. These early experiences are frequently cited as the origin of his lifelong work against caste discrimination.

The family later moved to Bombay, where he completed his schooling and studied at Elphinstone College, graduating from the University of Bombay in 1912 with economics and political science. He was one of the first students from an untouchable community to complete a college degree in the Bombay Presidency.
